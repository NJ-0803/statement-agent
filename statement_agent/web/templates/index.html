You can add PDF statements; spreadsheets (CSV, TSV, Excel .xlsx/.xls, OpenDocument .ods); bank downloads (OFX/QFX, QIF, MT940); JSON or XML exports; statements saved as Word or web pages (.docx, .html); or a clear photo (JPG or PNG). Up to 25 MB each.

    Your files stay on this computer. Scanned pages and photos are read by Claude through the Anthropic API; nothing is added to your statements until you say so.
